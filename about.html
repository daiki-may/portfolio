About Me

Biography

I am currently enrolled in the Ph.D course at Nara Institute of Science and Technology (NAIST) and Ulm University (Germany). My research focuses on systems that support changes in purchasing behavior and on changing behaviors towards healthier dietary habits.

Specifically, I am working on the development of olfactory interfaces to help reduce sugar intake, and voice dialogue systems for food knowledge. I am driven by the goal of realizing smart cities and smart lifestyles through ubiquitous computing.
